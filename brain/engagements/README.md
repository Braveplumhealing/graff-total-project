# Engagements — deliberately not here

**Rule (2026-08-31): client engagement material NEVER lives in this repo.**

`graff-total-project` is a **public** GitHub repository. Everything in `brain/` is readable
by anyone on the internet. That is fine for Brave Plum's own method, voice, decisions, and
plans — it is not fine for client work.

Client engagements involve real organizations and real people, often at their worst moment.
Facilitation and mediation carry **absolute confidentiality, in both directions.** A client
name, a presenting problem, an interview theme, or even a date paired with an industry can
identify people who never consented to being written down in public.

**Where it lives instead:** `~/Documents/BravePlum-Vault/engagements/` — the same private
vault that holds the manuscript and The Tempering curriculum. Owner-read-only.

**What may appear in this public repo:**
- the *shape* of the service (Mediation & Facilitation, already on the site)
- durable method, only if Johnny decides the method should be public — his call, not ours
- **never:** client names, sponsors, participants, interview content, themes notes,
  quotes, dates tied to an identifiable client, or anything derived from them

**No client story appears in any talk, page, post, or testimonial** without that client's
written permission — and even then, only in the words they approved.

*Related: `brain/decisions.md` (confidentiality guardrails) · `brain/speaking/conflict-as-catalyst.md`
(hard rule: no mediation-table stories, ever, even anonymized).*
