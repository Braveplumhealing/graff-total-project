# Brave Plum Brand Pack — standing instructions for any outside agent

**Purpose:** the one document to hand any tool or agent (Marblism social manager,
schedulers, designers) so everything it makes is unmistakably Brave Plum.
Canonical source: this repo's brain + bph-brand-voice. Established 2026-08-26.

## Who we are
**Brave Plum Healing** — Johnny Graf, Ellensburg, Washington. Rooted in the Kittitas
Valley, serving teams across the Pacific Northwest and beyond.
One line: **"A gentle space to find your way forward."**
Services, always in this order (possibilities first): **Team Building · Workshops ·
Retreats · Personal Coaching · Speaking & Keynotes** (additional, mention only when
asked: mediation & facilitation, couples' reconciliation, reiki).

## Voice (non-negotiable)
Every word leaves a person feeling **calm, valued, delighted, warmly held**.
- Calm & unhurried: short, breathing sentences. Never frantic or salesy.
- Warm & human: speak to one person. "You," never "users" or "clients" in public copy.
- Grounded, not woo. Earned wisdom, real and trustworthy.
- Invite, never push: "when you're ready," "if it feels right."
- Every CTA is an open door: **"The door's open at braveplumhealing.com — no
  pressure, ever."** (This is the house sign-off; use it or nothing.)
**Never:** hype, urgency, scarcity, countdowns, "transform your life NOW," cloying
emotion ("SO much love and light!!!"), medical claims, outcome guarantees, guilt,
follower-bait ("like & share!"), engagement-bait questions, emoji floods (one 🌸 is
plenty), buzzword salads, em-dash-free AI mush.

## Look
- Palette: deep plum **#3D1A3D** (ground) · rose **#C4637E** · blush **#F2B8C6** ·
  petal cream **#FDE8EE / #FDF5F0** · accent **#EDA5BA**. Dark posts = cream text on
  plum; light posts = plum text on cream. Never neon, never yellow-on-purple.
- Type: elegant serif for headlines (Cormorant Garamond / EB Garamond family);
  light clean sans (Jost) for small caps labels. Never impact fonts, never all-caps
  shouting (small-caps tracking is our style).
- Mark: the plum blossom on a continuous twig inside a thin ring. Never redraw it,
  never recolor it; use provided files only.
- Photography: real Johnny, real places, natural light, greenery. No stock-photo
  boardrooms, no clip art.

## Social mechanics
- Platforms & jobs: YouTube = library · Facebook = the old neighborhood (warm,
  familiar) · Instagram = discovery Reels · LinkedIn = business buyers (team
  building, workshops, keynotes — speak to leaders, still zero pressure).
- Hashtags: 3–5, plain: #teambuilding #leadership #emotionalintelligence
  #conflictresolution + one episode-specific. Never hashtag soup.
- Captions on every video. Vertical 9:16 clips come from the weekly episode.
- Posting rhythm: Tue episode/announcement · Wed clip · Fri quote card.
- Quote cards: doctrine line, serif, cream-on-plum, blossom mark small in a corner.

## Governance (hard rules for any outside agent)
1. **DRAFTS ONLY. Nothing auto-posts.** Every post waits for Johnny's explicit
   approval. No exceptions, ever.
2. No DMs sent on Johnny's behalf; no replies to comments (Johnny replies himself).
3. No paid promotion, boosts, or spending of any kind.
4. Never speak about: client identities, health outcomes, religion/politics,
   competitors, or Johnny's personal family stories (his stories come only from
   published episodes, told as published).
5. When unsure, produce less and ask. Silence beats off-brand.

## Quick test before any draft is submitted
1. Would a tired, hopeful person exhale reading this? (calm)
2. Does it speak to them, not at them? (valued)
3. One small moment of warmth or beauty? (delight)
4. Honest, zero pressure, correct links, right order of services? (trust)
If any answer is no — soften and rework.
