# Playbook — Video Marketing: A Walk Around the Block goes to town

**Purpose:** every episode Johnny films reaches the most people with the least
extra work, sounds like Brave Plum everywhere it lands, and quietly walks
viewers toward the door: braveplumhealing.com. Established 2026-08-25.

## The one rule that runs everything: FILM ONCE, SERVE MANY
One 3–5 minute episode becomes, automatically, in this order:
1. **YouTube** — the full episode (canonical home; the link that gets shared)
2. **2–3 Shorts/Reels clips** — 30–60s cuts of the sharpest moments (the doctrine
   line, the story beat, the practice) — same file works for YT Shorts, IG Reels,
   FB Reels (we already film vertical 9:16 — born for this)
3. **A Journal post** on braveplumhealing.com — cleaned transcript + embedded video
4. **A quote card** — the episode's doctrine line in Brave Plum petal style (for
   FB/IG/LinkedIn image posts; slowest to make, highest to share)
5. **A Mini Lesson** ($2.28) when the teaching earns one — buy link under the video
Johnny's only job in all of this: film the take, and tap "post" where posting
requires his account. Mr Rogers cuts, captions, writes, and preps everything else.

| *(later)* Email | The letter home. When ~100 subscribers exist. | "Letters from the Block" — episode + one paragraph. Not yet. |

## Cadence (sustainable > ambitious)
- **One episode per week.** Film whenever; PUBLISH on a fixed day —
  **Tuesday morning** (business audience is at their desk; "trigger Tuesday"
  energy without saying so). Same day every week = the show people count on.
- Rollout rhythm for each episode:
  - Tue: YouTube full + Journal post + LinkedIn
  - Wed: Facebook native + first Reel/Short
  - Fri: second Short + quote card everywhere
- Miss a week? Say nothing, post next Tuesday. No apologizing to the algorithm.

## Voice rules on socials (bph-brand-voice applies everywhere)
- Never pushy. The CTA is always an open door, not a hook: "the door's open at
  braveplumhealing.com — no pressure, ever."
- Captions on every video, every platform (most watch silent).
- Hashtags: 3–5, plain (#teambuilding #leadership #emotionalintelligence
  #conflictresolution + one episode-specific). No hashtag soup.
- Reply to every real comment for the first months. The neighborhood is built
  one reply at a time. Never argue with trolls — triggers are teachers, we teach.
- Never buy ads, followers, or engagement without an explicit Johnny decision
  (money = Tier 2 anyway).

## The funnel, in Brave Plum language
Video (free teaching) → braveplumhealing.com (the neighborhood) →
$2.28 Mini Lesson (a customer and a little coin) → 228 Thread coaching /
Team Building & Workshops inquiry (the living). Speaking clips later feed the
Speaking & Keynotes door. Every video description carries the same block:
> A short lesson from Johnny Graf of Brave Plum Healing. Team building,
> workshops & retreats — the door's open at braveplumhealing.com
> (+ mini-lesson link when one exists)

## Launch order (start here)
1. **YouTube channel** — concierge walk, Johnny's clicks (account = Tier 2).
   Name: A Walk Around the Block · Brave Plum Healing. Emblem avatar, hero-line
   banner. Upload Episode №1 with the finished thumbnail.
2. **Journal post for Episode №1** — embed + $2.28 buy button (the pilot's
   missing piece goes live).
3. **Videos page returns as "A Walk Around the Block"** with the episode + a
   subscribe link.
4. **Facebook + Instagram + LinkedIn**: post Episode №1 natively, announce the
   show ONCE ("I used to walk this block live — the show is back, weekly").
5. Cut 2 Shorts from Episode №1; begin the weekly rhythm with Episode №2.

## Measures that matter (check monthly, 15 minutes, no obsessing)
- Conversations started (comments, DMs, emails, calls booked) — the real number
- Mini-lesson sales (customers, not revenue — $2.28 is a handshake)
- Subscribers/followers (direction, not size)
- Site visits from video (GoatCounter, once wired)
Vanity metrics are not neighbors. A hundred true neighbors beats ten thousand
passersby.

## Division of labor (Hands Covenant intact)
- **Johnny:** films; approves; taps post/upload on his accounts; replies as himself.
- **Mr Rogers:** episode assembly (show playbook), Shorts cuts, thumbnails, quote
  cards, descriptions, Journal posts, buy buttons, calendars, monthly measure
  digest. All packaged and handed over for one-tap.
- Account creation, logins, payments, ads: Tier 2 — Johnny's hands only, forever.
