# Download — The Path of Love (the lightning bolt)

**Date:** 2026-08-29, morning. Johnny: "I had a lightning bolt of clarity last
night. This space is intended to describe that clarity and put your team to work.
I'll need the whole neighborhood."

**Capture rule:** verbatim, as it arrives. Organization comes after the pouring.

---

## Part one — the guiding principle (verbatim)

"It all starts from a walk around the block that goes something like this.

I have a simple philosophy that has become my guiding principle. It is that we
come from love, we return to love, and most importantly that our work while on
our earthly journey is to nurture a path of love."

---

## Part two — the unpacking, the well, the gift, the precipice (verbatim)

"When this philosophy first started to thread itself into my ways of being it was
a difficult thing to unpack. At first I that nurturing a path of love meant loving
every human I encountered. To love more humans. As you can imagine - I was
exhausted. And with that exhaustion came frustration and resentment. And so I had
to put down this whole nurturing a path of love idea and simply recover. I had
depleted myself moving through life without healthy boundaries. I wonder if you
have ever felt that way. Depleted and exhausted and unable to share your gift.
But then as time went by I picked the idea back up of nurturing a path of love
and gave it another go. This time I thought that nurturing a path forward meant
loving harder - and so I loved harder with more intensity and focus. As you can
probably guess it led to the same result - exhausted and depleted. More
iterations came along the way - each one ending with resentment and confusion.
Confusion because I knew that the information that was given - to nurture a path
of love - was pure and right.

But it is said that the past is perfect - it brings us to this transformational
moment. And so here we are in this moment and I the puzzle finally unlocked
itself. The love that I was intended to nurture was never for anyone else but
myself. Because once you figure out how to love yourself your well - the place
that you go to dip into energy and vitality resides within you. Nurturing a path
of love in turn tends to our own garden. You flourish, and blossom, and create
vitality in yourself. And once you learn how to "garden" yourself, you will
never forget.

You will always be able to fill yourself back up with the strength that you need
to be yourself and express your gift. And the world needs your gift. Because your
gift was given to you on purpose. It is no accident that you have this thing that
lives inside of you waiting to be expressed. By sharing this idea, I am
expressing my own gift - connecting humans and helping them to see the light
that resides within them.

And I believe that your gift is intended for you from the divine. I use the word
divine on purpose here. We could use a multitude of other words - God comes to
mind. But God is also associated with organized religions and many of us have
been traumatized from organized religions - and so divine seems like a neutral
word to use to keep us all in this conversation. Because this is an important
conversation.

It's because I believe that we stand on a precipice, the last edge of the not
knowing, and the beginning of an awakening that we have not yet experienced in
our humanity. I believe that very soon many of us are going to look at one
another and ask the question, "Why did we hate each other". We are all beautiful
and strong. We are all facing challenges and difficulties that are both uniquely
our own and understood by the collective.

In this way, self love is the defining moment of our time. In fact, I would go
so far as saying that our lives depend on it. Because if we keep dipping into
each other's wells without understanding that we carry the divine within us
through our gifts then our gifts will never be fully expressed."

## Part three — the assignment (verbatim)

"Something like that for the walk around the block.

But meanwhile, we get to build a workshop and all of the accroutements that
support it. During my walk around the block I will make mention of, 'if this
sounds like something you want to explore hop onto my website and sign up for
my workshop.'

But I want to have it already built and I want it to be spectacular.

I want the workshop to be at a pricepoint that allows anyone to participate and
also to value the magic of my work.

This is where your team comes in - tap into my brain - go and explore the work
of others that are doing work with self love and lets create a resourceful
workshop that helps people to dive into themselves and uncover their own beauty.

At the end of the workshop I want the participants to be able to look themselves
in the mirror and say, 'I love you ________'.

Are you up for the challenge? Is your team ready?"

## Part four — the design question (verbatim)

"My journey to self love was long and arduous - several decades and plenty of
scratches along the way. Some cuts too. Deep wounds. I'm curious if there is a
way through that is less painful."

*This is the workshop's reason for existing: Johnny's decades become the map so
others bleed less. The workshop IS the less-painful way through.*
