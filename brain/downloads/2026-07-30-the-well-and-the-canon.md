## Verbatim-faithful (lightly punctuated)

I have been thinking about my previous work as a principal and my exit from public
education. There came a moment of realization that I was doing more harm than good.
Public education has become a device that unfortunately represses our god-given gift.
We are so busy assessing, cataloging, critiquing our children that they have exactly
zero opportunity to explore their magic. And appreciating the magic of the child is
well beyond the job description for educators these days. Which is crazy, because our
work should be doing one of two things, or both: education should either be
enlightening the individual — letting them understand their light/magic — or it should
provide society with some benefit. In a perfect world the enlightened human should
serve the needs of humanity through sharing their gift to the world.

And what is the consequence of a human not knowing their gift? Or worse yet, knowing
their gift but not being able to share it because they are busy with homework? Or even
worse — knowing their gift and expressing their gift and being criticized for it
because it doesn't fit in the box created by public education, and the school being
judged because the student is not performing on state assessments? The consequence is a
disconnect from themselves and the magic that they possess. Depression, addiction, low
self-esteem, resentment, mental illness, violent ideation, broken relationships,
burnout, lack of efficacy — the list goes on and on.

**Our gift is our light, and I believe the well from which we are supposed to dip for
our self-admiration and self-love.** I can guarantee you that self-love is not a part
of any curriculum in public education. And so we chase each other around trying to find
a new well to dip into, because we were never told that we have an abundant well
waiting for us 24/7/365. But instead we find other ways to fill the void. We shop, we
drink, we numb.

There is a reason why we are worshiping narcissistic leaders and bowing to the mega
corporations while acquiescing our responsibilities to one another to influencers that
keep us entertained and chained to our devices. I am not a conspiracy theorist by any
means, but this is all working out for those in power and the ultra wealthy — seems
quite Orwellian to me. And counter to what I believe we are meant to be.

I sometimes wonder if our gift from the universe/god/the divine is meant to be paid
back. Our gift lights us up, and in turn we carry our light forward through the dark of
the universe. **How can we be a torch if we never strike the match?** I think we are
doing this wrong. **I believe the canon of our time is to awaken to self-love, and if
we do so humanity is going to ascend to a height that we have not yet reached.**

## Distillation (the load-bearing pieces)
- **The two legitimate purposes of education:** enlighten the individual (their
  light/magic) and/or benefit society — perfected when the enlightened human serves
  humanity through their gift. (A crisp, teachable thesis.)
- **THE WELL (new doctrine):** the gift IS the well of self-love — self-admiration is
  meant to be drawn from what you're for. Void-filling (shopping, drinking, numbing,
  influencer-worship) = dipping in other wells because no one told us we have our own,
  24/7/365. This LINKS the book's Part 2 (Love Thyself) to Parts 3–4 (gift/serve): you
  love yourself by drinking from your own well.
- **The consequence cascade:** gift-disconnect → depression, addiction, low self-esteem,
  resentment, mental illness, violent ideation, broken relationships, burnout.
- **The match line (keeper):** "How can we be a torch if we never strike the match?"
- **The canon line (keeper):** "The canon of our time is to awaken to self-love."
- **Open question he's holding:** is the gift meant to be *paid back*? (Ch7 of his own
  book answers: stewardship, not debt — paid FORWARD, not back.)
- Societal layer: keep the critique incentive-shaped, not conspiratorial (his own
  instinct — "not a conspiracy theorist").
