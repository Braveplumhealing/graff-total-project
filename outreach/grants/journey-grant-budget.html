Project Budget — “The Journey to Self Love”

A collection of poems and stories — City of Ellensburg Artist Grant

Project Summary

“The Journey to Self Love” is a collection of original poems and stories that trace the road from self-judgment to self-acceptance — giving words to what so many people carry and cannot say. This grant supports completing the collection and producing a small printed book edition, so the work can be placed directly into the hands of readers and families in the Ellensburg community.
