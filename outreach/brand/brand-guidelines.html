01 · The Mark

The blossom on the twig

One plum blossom, carried on a continuous twig, held inside a thin ring — captured and displayed. It is the signature of everything Brave Plum makes.

Rules

  • Never redraw, recolor, rotate, or stretch the mark.
  • Use the supplied files only (light & dark versions).
  • Clear space around it: at least half the ring’s width on all sides.
  • Minimum size: 24 px / 0.25 in — smaller and the petals blur.
  • Light version on cream/pale grounds; dark version on plum grounds. Never on photos busier than soft greenery.

02 · Color

Plum ground, petal light

Deep Plum#3D1A3D · the ground
Twig Plum#7A3070 · depth & quotes
Rose#C4637E · buttons & accents
Accent Petal#EDA5BA · highlights
Blush#F2B8C6 · lines & labels
Petal Cream#FDE8EE / #FDF5F0 · light ground

How to combine

  • Dark pieces: cream text (#FDE8EE) on Deep Plum; blush for small labels.
  • Light pieces: Deep Plum text on Petal Cream; rose for links and buttons.
  • Rose buttons always carry cream text — never rose-on-rose.
  • Never: neon, pure black, pure white, gold foil looks, or yellow on purple.

03 · Typography

A serif that breathes, a sans that whispers

Headlines — Cormorant Garamond (or EB Garamond)

Weight 400–500, generous line height, sentence case. Italic for warmth and doctrine lines.

Labels & body — Jost (light)

04 · Voice

Calm · Valued · Delight · Trust

Every word leaves a person feeling calm, valued, delighted, and warmly held — met exactly where they are, with no pressure to be anyone else. Speak to one person, kindly. Short, breathing sentences. Grounded, never woo.

Always

  • Lead with the person’s experience, not the offer.
  • Invite: “when you’re ready,” “if it feels right.”
  • Be specific and honest about what a thing actually is.
  • Plum, petals, sanctuary, seasons, coming home.
  • Close with the house sign-off (below).

Never

  • Hype, urgency, scarcity, countdowns.
  • “Transform your life NOW” — or any shouting.
  • Cloying emotion or emoji floods (one 🌸 is plenty).
  • Medical claims or promised outcomes.
  • Engagement-bait, guilt, pressure of any kind.

The house sign-off

07 · For Agents & Partners

The hard rules

Governance — non-negotiable

  • Drafts only. Nothing auto-posts. Every post waits for Johnny’s explicit approval.
  • No DMs on Johnny’s behalf; comment replies are Johnny’s alone.
  • No paid promotion or spending of any kind.
  • Never invent links, prices, offers, discounts, or testimonials.
  • Off-limits topics: client identities, health outcomes, religion & politics, competitors, Johnny’s family stories beyond what published episodes tell.
  • When unsure: produce less and ask. Silence beats off-brand.

Before any draft ships — the quick test

  • Would a tired, hopeful person exhale reading this? (calm)
  • Does it speak to them, not at them? (valued)
  • One small moment of warmth or beauty? (delight)
  • Honest, zero pressure, links and service order correct? (trust)

If any answer is no — soften and rework.
