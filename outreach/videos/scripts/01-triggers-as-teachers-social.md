# Script 01 — Social adaptations ("film once, serve three meals")

Film the FULL script once. Everything below is cut or written from that single take.
Governing rule (Johnny, 2026-07-29): *"I do not desire to chase the dollar — rather I
desire the dollars to find me on purpose."* → honest hooks, no clickbait, no engagement
bait, few hashtags. Findable, never chasing.

---

## 1 · The 60-second Reel / Short / TikTok cut (~150 words)

Social starts mid-story — the hook must land in 3 seconds. In Descript, cut the full
take down to these lines (or film this as its own quick take if easier):

**On-screen text, first 3 seconds:** *The man screaming at my windshield turned out to be a teacher.*

> Two flaggers at a construction site.
> One waved me through.
> The other was screaming at my windshield — arms going like a windmill.
>
> We had happy music playing in the car.
> I did **not** feel happy.
> My caveman self wanted to stop the car and show him who's boss.
>
> It took me a few miles to settle — and finally ask:
> *what was in that moment for me to learn?*
>
> Here's what I know about triggers:
> they don't point at the person in front of you.
> They point at something **in you** that's asking to be seen.
> That man didn't create what happened in me. He just **found** it.
>
> So when the heat rises: **Pause.**
> Name it: *"Something in me just got touched."*
> And ask: *"What are you here to teach me?"*
>
> You don't have to like the teacher.
> You just have to stay for the lesson.

*(Cut for time if needed: the happy-music beat survives everything — it's the charm.)*

## 3 · LinkedIn text post (standalone — no video needed, workplace angle)

> Two flaggers gave me opposite directions at a construction site last week.
> One waved me through. The other screamed at my windshield.
>
> Every team has this moment. Two people, two directions, rising heat — and everyone's
> story writing itself at full speed.
>
> I teach teams a distinction I hold in my own work:
> bravery faces down the danger around you; courage transforms the world within you.
> A trigger feels like outer danger. That's the disguise. It's usually pointing at
> something inside — an old story, a crossed boundary, a thing asking to be seen.
>
> The most useful question in a heated room isn't "who's right?"
> It's the student's question: "what is this here to teach me?"
>
> It took me a few miles on the freeway to ask it. Your team can learn to ask it faster.
>
> — Johnny Graf · Brave Plum Healing · team building, workshops & retreats

## Posting notes

- **Vertical 9:16** for Reels/Shorts/TikTok; **captions always on** (most social viewing
  is sound-off — Descript auto-captions).
- **Native uploads** — post the video file to each platform directly, never a link to
  YouTube (platforms bury outbound links).
- **First line = the hook.** Everything above the "…more" fold must earn the tap.
- **Cadence, one story three meals:** YouTube full version → Reel cut a day later →
  LinkedIn text later that week. One filming session feeds a whole week.
- **Link discipline:** braveplumhealing.com in the IG bio (captions can't link);
  in-text for FB/LinkedIn.
- **Comments:** reply as yourself, warmly, no selling. The brand is presence — the
  comment section is where it shows.
