A few days ago I sat down with Dr. David Douglas for a real conversation — not a
highlight reel, just an hour of two people who've known each other a long time,
talking honestly about the things that actually change a life.

We covered a lot of ground: leaving twenty-five years in public education on
purpose, reclaiming my own name after years of losing myself inside a title, the
difference between bravery and courage, and what it actually takes to stay
grounded once people start calling you "healer."

If you've got an hour, it's worth the whole listen. If you're short on time, the
short version is this: **you don't need a crisis to change your life. You just
need permission.**
